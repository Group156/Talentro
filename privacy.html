Privacy

Clear handling of your information.

This notice explains what happens when you contact Talentro or apply to join the expert network through talentro.tech.

Who is responsible

Talentro is the data controller for information submitted through this website. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to talentrotech@gmail.com.

What we collect

For customer enquiries, we collect your name, work email, company, required expertise and project details. For expert applications, we collect your name, email, country, field, qualification and expected rate.

Why we use it

We use the information to assess and respond to customer requirements, evaluate expert fit, contact you about relevant work and maintain a record of the resulting business relationship. We do not sell submitted information.

How it is handled

Submissions are stored in Talentro's controlled systems and may be processed by infrastructure and email providers acting on Talentro's instructions. Access is limited to people who need it for the stated purpose.

Retention and your rights

We retain information only while it remains relevant to the enquiry, application or resulting relationship, subject to legal record-keeping duties. You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to its use, by contacting the address above.
